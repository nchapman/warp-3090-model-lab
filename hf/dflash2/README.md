# Qwen3.5-4B-DFlash2

DFlash2 speculative drafter for [Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B).
One recipe, one checkpoint: the end-of-training drafter (epoch 6 of 6),
selected at the serving temperature.

## Acceptance (τ — interval-weighted, 20-prompt chat ladder; GB10 serving)

| config | τ, greedy (t=0) | τ, t=0.7 (serving) | τ, t=1.0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Qwen3.5-4B-W4A16-GPTQ](https://huggingface.co/nchapman/Qwen3.5-4B-W4A16-GPTQ) verifier (training verifier) | 4.05 (n=2) | **3.79** (n=4: 3.71–3.85) | 3.25 (n=2) |
| Qwen3.5-4B bf16 base | — | **3.75** | 3.32 |
| native MTP k=3 (baseline, same ladder) | 2.99 | 2.86 | 2.66 |

Beats the model's native MTP by **~+1 τ at every temperature** in every
measured config, and drafts against the bf16 base as well as against the
W4A16-GPTQ quant it was trained on.

**Measurement honesty:** τ depends on sampling temperature and carries
run-to-run spread (t=1.0 is heavy-tailed: 0.34 observed on identical inputs;
t=0.7 spread ~0.1). Numbers above are reported with n and ranges, never as
single draws. Ranking is temperature-dependent — see Selection.

## Selection

All six epochs' checkpoints were ranked at three temperatures with repeats.
At the serving point (t=0.7 — the same sampling config the training corpus
was generated with, temp 0.7 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 20), τ rises monotonically
to the end of training: 3.28 → 3.49 → 3.68 → 3.79 (n=4 on the top two). The
shipped artifact is the end-of-training checkpoint — no early stop.

Under greedy decoding the picture inverts: a mid-training snapshot (epoch 4)
peaks at 4.20 vs end-of-training 4.05. Greedy-heavy workloads (agentic,
extraction) may prefer an earlier snapshot; for sampled serving the
end-of-training drafter is the measured winner.

## Recipe

- Corpus: 260M tokens — 100k Nemotron-Cascade-2 conversations with assistant
  turns **regenerated by the deployed W4A16-GPTQ quant** at the serving
  sampling config (t=0.7 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 20, seeded), so the drafter
  learns exactly what its verifier emits where it matters; 6-tap hidden states
- Training: lr 6e-4, cosine schedule, 4% warmup, 6 epochs; block 16 /
  15 spec tokens
- Data lineage: slice ids frozen in
  [warp-3090-quant-lab-frozen-data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nchapman/warp-3090-quant-lab-frozen-data);
  corpus rebuildable via the
  [warp-3090-quant-lab](https://github.com/nchapman/warp-3090-quant-lab) pipeline

## Requirements

speculators 0.8.1 · vllm 0.31.0 · transformers 5.16.1 · torch 2.13.0
