`in_proj_a/b` (3.9M params, 0.1% of model) are W4-packed, while every

  +3.36 / wKL 0.1094 vs champion +3.20 / 0.1087 — the SGLang/Marlin-era
  script changes cost 0.16pt dPPL, well inside the 0.8–1.7pt rebuild
  drift band. The requested current-script nemotron-calib rebuild is
  probe-equivalent to the shipped champion.**
- Champion is vLLM-servable but NOT SGLang/Marlin-safe (N=32 a/b
  packed) — matters if the deployment target ever changes.

## Finding 2 — dPPL ranking across calib recipes is inside rebuild noise

Same-recipe, same-calib-sha rebuilds on this box moved dPPL by
0.8–1.7pt (with wKL stable to ±0.002): **dPPL margins below ~2pt
between single builds are not evidence of recipe ordering.** The
wKL band 0.109–0.113 across champion/qwenfinals/if3x/4bself is tight
and champion-led, but the spread itself is near the metric's
rebuild stability — treat all four as wKL-equivalent pending
multi-build replicates.
